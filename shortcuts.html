YT Silence Cutter

Keyboard shortcuts

Every shortcut is Alt + Shift plus one key.

Why Alt + Shift? YouTube binds bare keys (k, j, l, space, arrows, 0–9) and the browser owns Ctrl/Cmd combinations. Requiring Alt + Shift means these never collide with either — and they are ignored while you are typing in a text box.
